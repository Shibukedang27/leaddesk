## Capture Checklist

- Use seeded demo data only.
- Hide JWTs, database URLs, email inboxes, browser extensions, and private bookmarks.
- Keep browser chrome out of screenshots unless intentionally presenting responsive frames.
- Capture in light mode because dark mode is intentionally disabled for now.
- Prefer 1440x1000 desktop captures and 390x844 mobile captures.
- Compress PNGs before committing.

## Suggested Banner Direction

Design a calm, premium SaaS banner:

- Left: `LeadDesk Mini` and `Modern Lead Management CRM`.
- Right: blurred-but-readable dashboard screenshot in a browser frame.
- Background: off-white surface with subtle blue accents.
- Avoid heavy gradients, fake devices, or busy marketing decoration.
