# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in LeadDesk Mini respectful, inclusive, and professional.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and considerate language.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Keep discussions relevant, practical, and kind.
- Protect private data, credentials, and user information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing secrets, tokens, passwords, production data, or private logs.
- Repeated off-topic comments that disrupt maintainers or contributors.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this code of conduct.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through the repository owner profile.
